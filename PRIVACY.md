# BeatWave Music Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

This Privacy Policy explains how BeatWave Music, a Discord music bot maintained by KeuqS, processes information when you use the bot.

## Information processed

When you use BeatWave Music, the bot may receive and process information made available through the Discord API, including Discord user IDs, usernames or display names, server and channel IDs, voice-state information, command names, command options, music search queries, and technical error or operational logs.

The bot does not intentionally request or store Discord passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or audio recordings. Do not send secrets or sensitive personal information in bot commands.

## How information is used

Information is used to respond to commands, provide music playback and queue features, verify that voice commands are used in the appropriate channel, maintain reliability, troubleshoot errors, and protect the bot from abuse.

BeatWave Music does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and by infrastructure or music-source services used to provide playback, such as Lavalink or the selected music source. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

## Retention and security

BeatWave Music is designed to minimize stored information. Operational logs may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, and service operation. No retention period is guaranteed for data processed by Discord or third-party services.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the bot and its configuration, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and requests

If you want to ask about information processed by BeatWave Music or request deletion of information held by the maintainer, open an issue at https://github.com/KeuqS/discord-music-bot/issues. Requests may require enough information to identify the relevant server, user, or command without asking you to disclose passwords or tokens.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot or its data practices change. The latest version will be published in this file.
